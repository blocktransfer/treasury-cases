## I. Purpose and Scope

- Identify the IRS collection notice and tax periods at issue.
- Request records supporting the asserted liabilities and proposed levy.
- Request documentation relevant to the verification required by 26 USC 6330(c)(1).
- Explain that the requested materials are necessary to evaluate and respond to the IRS's determinations.
- Request that records be provided through written correspondence.

## II. Tax Account and Assessment Records

Request, for each disputed tax period:

- Complete account transcripts, including transaction codes and associated dates.
- Available assessment records, including Forms 4340 or equivalent assessment documentation.
- Identification of each assessment, its amount, date, and tax period.
- Records of adjustments, abatements, reversals, payments, and credits.
- Identification of the statutory authority underlying each assessed tax or penalty.
- Available supporting computations and explanations of the amounts assessed.

## III. Notices and Proof of Mailing

- Copies of all relevant notices of deficiency, assessment, balance due, and intent to levy.
- Records identifying the dates each notice was generated and mailed.
- Available certified-mail records, tracking information, and delivery records.
- Records establishing the taxpayer's last known address when each notice was issued.
- Any returned or undeliverable correspondence.
- Records establishing any prior opportunity to dispute the underlying liabilities.

## IV. Underlying Liability Documentation

- Copies of returns and amended returns relevant to the disputed assessments.
- Records of adjustments made by the IRS.
- Examination reports and proposed adjustment explanations, if applicable.
- Relevant penalty determinations and supporting materials.
- Records supporting the calculation of statutory interest.
- Documents establishing the factual and legal basis for each disputed liability.

## V. Collection and Levy Records

- Records identifying the proposed levy and the liabilities it concerns.
- Relevant collection account histories.
- Available records of collection determinations and authorizations.
- Documentation of compliance with required pre-levy notice procedures.
- Records relating to any applicable suspension or restriction on collection.
- Records used to calculate the remaining collection limitations period, including any claimed suspension or extension.

## VI. Administrative and Appeals Records

- The collection administrative file transmitted to Appeals.
- Relevant case history entries and correspondence.
- Documents provided by Collection to Appeals concerning the disputed liabilities or proposed levy.
- Records relied upon to verify compliance with applicable legal and administrative requirements.
- Relevant nonprivileged documents materially considered by Appeals.
- Any supplementary investigation results or factual submissions received during the proceeding.

## VII. Format and Delivery

- Request electronic copies where available, or paper copies by mail.
- Request that any record identified but not provided be listed, with an explanation of its unavailability or withholding.
- Request identification of the office holding records that cannot be produced by Appeals.
- Request that any clarifications or requests to narrow the scope be made in writing.
- Request that correspondence be sent to the taxpayer's stated mailing address.

## VIII. Opportunity to Review and Respond

- Request disclosure of responsive records sufficiently before a final determination to allow meaningful review.
- Request a reasonable period to submit written evidence and legal arguments after receiving the records.
- Request an opportunity to respond to new information materially relied upon by Appeals.
- Request that Appeals address material procedural and substantive objections raised in the written record.

## IX. Closing Request

The taxpayer requests that the IRS provide the identified records to the extent available and lawfully disclosable, identify any withheld or unavailable materials, and provide a reasonable opportunity for written review and response before issuing its Collection Due Process determination.
