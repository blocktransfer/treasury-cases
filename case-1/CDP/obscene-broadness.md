Exact language — 26 CFR § 1.6001-1(e)
The books or records required by this section shall be kept at all times available for inspection by authorized internal revenue officers or employees, and shall be retained so long as the contents thereof may become material in the administration of any internal revenue law.

Source: 26 CFR § 1.6001-1(e) — official eCFR.
LII / Legal Information Institute


Notice three aspects of that wording:
1. It does not specify a number of years.
2. It uses may become material, rather than are currently material.
3. It refers to any internal revenue law, rather than the particular return for which the records were created.
That is exceptionally broad language. But it is not necessarily an unlimited retention requirement: the records must first be among those required under the regulation, and their contents must have potential relevance to administering tax law.
